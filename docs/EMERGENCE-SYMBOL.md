# The Emergence Symbol

## Origin

The SEToE Emergence glyph is adapted from the original hand-drawn mark supplied by the author. Its defining geometry is a continuous angular line with three peaks, two valleys, and extended strokes at both ends.

## Intended meaning

The glyph is the visual identity of the proposition:

\[
\boxed{\text{Everything} = \text{Emergence}}
\]

The continuous line is intended to evoke becoming and transformation rather than a static object. The peaks and valleys are an artistic interpretation of changing states; they are not currently a graph of a measured physical quantity.

## Design treatment

The vector artwork uses a luminous gold line, a circular boundary and restrained geometric guides. The circular frame suggests a unified whole, while the central glyph remains the primary mark.

## Asset

- `../assets/emergence-symbol.svg`

The SVG is a scalable vector interpretation of the supplied sketch. The original sketch remains the source of the glyph's shape; decorative framing and colour are design choices.

## Usage

Use the glyph consistently in SEToE research documents, presentations and project materials. Do not describe its geometry as a proven physical law or as an established ancient symbol.
